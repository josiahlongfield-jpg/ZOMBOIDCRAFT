Harvestman

Round 6, from your armoured render, in near-black armour with a bronze tint. From the front the body is a downward arrowhead: long creased blade plates sweep out of a centre keel, jagged spines crown the back, and a beard of shards hangs under the point. A row of blade spines runs along the spine from head to tail, small at the head, tallest mid-back, small again at the tail. Feeding, its scythes go in through the victim's back and out of its chest and drag it up to the mouth, where the beard covers the top of its head, and every red line glows. Each leg has a third joint, an ankle; press Creep to see them move. Press Test room to let it loose in a room: it climbs, hides, drags the body and decides everything for itself, and a tap anywhere in the room makes a noise it can hear.

  • A sleek dart of a body in near-black, bronze-tinted armour: long creased blade plates in a herringbone off a centre keel with a thin red seam, jagged spines crowning the back, a row of blade spines along the spine (small at the head, tallest mid-back, small at the tail) and a beard of shards under the point.
  • Four long armoured legs with three joints each: a thigh up to a capped cog knee high above the body, a shin down to a cog ankle, then a long shingled point. Overlapping blade plates cover every segment.
  • No visible head: the armoured front is its face, closing to a point under the front.
  • Two small scythe arms folded under the head like a mantis's, and red sinew and black cables looping out to the legs.
