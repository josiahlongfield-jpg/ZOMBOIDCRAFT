ZomboidCraft trailer

A 75-second teaser built from what is in the playtest jar today. It starts quiet and lonely in the endless night, builds through looting and the first kill, then lets everything loose: the horde, limbs coming off, jets overhead and a last stand on the street. Each shot lists the exact setup commands, where to film it and how to move the camera.

Before you film

Every session

  • Use a world with cheats on (all /zc commands need them) on the newest playtest jar.
  • Photon on, with Mie fog turned up a little so the mod's fog shows. Render distance 16 or more for the wide shots.
  • Run /zc cinematic. The HUD and green dots go but the gun stays in your hands, zombies stop noticing you, and you can't be hurt. Chat still shows while you type.
  • Turn the in-game music slider to zero (you'll add your own track) and keep the other sounds up.
  • Press F1 only for shots with no hands at all, such as Replay Mod fly-throughs and empty streets.

Recording

  • Handheld shots (looting, shooting, the last stand): record live with OBS at 1440p60, CQP 18 to 20, or ShadowPlay at its highest bitrate.
  • Sweeping shots (city, horde, Glass Line, carrier): start a Replay Mod recording, play the moment out, then in the replay editor place keyframes and render the camera path at 60 fps.
  • Shoot every shot 3 or 4 times and pick the best take. Aim for 10 s of footage for every 3 s you'll use.
  • Let 2 s run before and after the action so the cuts have room.

Setup
/zc cinematic weather downpour /zc event siren

Record audio only, standing anywhere outside. Use 4 s of the siren, from the first moment it is clearly audible.

0:457 s

The horde comes up the street Replay Mod, low and wide

The biggest shot. A street packed kerb to kerb with the dead, all pouring toward the camera through rain and mist. Low angle, slow pull back.

Setup
/zc goto downtown /zc cinematic weather downpour /zc cinematic horde 120 40 /zc cinematic lure 96
Camera
Stand in the middle of a long straight street, facing down it. Zombies ignore you in cinematic mode, so they flood past and around you. Replay the moment and pull a camera backwards just above head height as they come. /zc perf shows the FPS cost; drop to 80 if Replay stutters.
Note
FPS 120 zombies is heavy. Replay renders offline, so the final video stays smooth even if the game doesn't.

Last stand Handheld, the climax

Back to the player on a street corner, laser on, firing full auto into the crowd as it closes in. Fast cuts between the muzzle, bodies falling and the crowd getting closer. The final cut is a zombie lunging into the lens.

Setup
/zc cinematic zombies normal /zc cinematic horde 60 24 /zc cinematic time midnight
Capture
Assault rifle or SMG on full auto (X), laser on (J). Ghost mode is off here so they really go for you. You can get hurt, so keep /zc heal ready between takes (creative won't work, because zombies ignore creative players). For the lunge, let one get right up close and stop recording on the hit.

/zc cinematic

CommandWhat it does
/zc cinematicClean HUD with the gun still in hand, no green dots, zombies ignore you, you can't be hurt.
/zc cinematic offPuts everything back: HUD, zombies, weather, clock and flying speed.
/zc cinematic hud clean|fullHide or show the HUD and dots on their own.
/zc cinematic zombies ghost|freeze|normalZombies ignore you (they still come to gunshots), every zombie stands still mid-step, or back to normal.
/zc cinematic horde [count] [distance]Spawns a horde up ahead where you're looking (default 40 at 28 blocks) and sends it your way.
/zc cinematic lure [radius]Every zombie in range (default 64) walks to you.
/zc cinematic time dusk|night|midnight|dawn|day|freeSets the clock and holds it there. free lets it run.
/zc cinematic weather <kind>|freeSets the weather and holds it: drizzle, rain, downpour, fog, gale, storm, thunderstorm, severe_storm.
/zc cinematic fly [speed|normal]Lets you fly even in survival, slowly (0.015 by default; normal is 0.05) for steady camera moves.
/zc cinematic smooth on|offEased mouse look for smooth handheld pans.
/zc cinematic helpLists these in chat.

Editing

Cut

  • DaVinci Resolve (free). Put a 1440p 60 fps timeline down and export at 1440p for YouTube; it gets the better bitrate.
  • Keep the opening slow with long shots. From 0:45 on, cut every 1 to 2 s, on the beat.
  • Drop all sound to silence for one beat right before the title. That moment of silence hits harder than any sound effect.
  • A light teal-and-amber grade suits the neon and wet streets. Don't brighten it: the darkness is the look.

Sound

  • Keep the game's own rain, gunshots and horde groans under the music. They sell it more than the track does.
  • Music: a slow, low drone that builds into a heavy hit at 0:45. Royalty-free sources: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, or the free tier on Uppbeat.
  • Credit the music in the video description if its licence asks for it.
