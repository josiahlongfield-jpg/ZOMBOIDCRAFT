# Purge plan: what needs a place in the Reach world
From the purge plan (https://claude.ai/artifact/92iDNQQ3vpJUMVT5gpcU8z), Josiah's answers 2026-11-08. Counts are starting numbers; sandbox settings tune most of them.

| Thing | How many | Placement rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base sites (R12) | 5 candidates (Sword Base default) | Each needs a BUILDABLE claim area, about 64x64 blocks: room for armoury, gear room (displays placed anywhere), Spartan booth, operator locker, ammo store, barracks, med bay, motor pool + hangar, command room, power. A flat Pelican/Falcon pad and a ground vehicle entrance. About 100 m of open approach on 2+ sides for the Covenant base-attack waves (Firefight layout around it). |
| Outposts | 2-3 candidate spots per district | Small buildable pad, about 24 blocks, beside a road. Covenant night raids hit these (not constantly). |
| UNSC supply pods (main ammo) | 8-12 drop zones per district | Open sky above (no roofs or overhangs), a 20 m clear radius, flat or gentle ground, not inside base claims. Spread across the district; some in contested spots. The pod draws hordes and a Covenant guard squad (Hunters on rare pods). |
| Elite drop pods from cruisers | Reuse the pod zones + Covenant-held plazas | Open sky; cruisers hang in the sky (fits R10). |
| Covenant supply pods | 3-5 per district | Inside or near Covenant-held ground. |
| Covenant forward base | 1 per district | Shield generator + 1-2 comms relays (destroying a relay stops reinforcements). Use the mission's own Covenant positions where possible. |
| Phantom/Spirit drop points | Use the mission data | They drop troops and herded zombies; they need clear air and landing ground. |
| Covenant vehicles | Ghost patrol routes on roads; 1-2 Wraith artillery spots in open districts (plains, farms); Banshee patrol air | Ghosts, Revenants and Wraiths are stealable, so park some reachable. |
| UNSC vehicles | 1-2 spawns per district + base motor pool | Warthog, Mongoose, Scorpion, Falcon. Pelican via cleared pads (fits R13). |
| District cache | 1 per district | Opens once when the district is purged. Indoors or in a bunker is fine. |
| High-value targets (Zealot, General) | 1-2 per district | A defended landmark (rooftop, spire plateau, bridge). Each carries a cache marker. |
| Noble Team rescues | 5 fixed spots, 1 each | Pinned down or captured by the Covenant. Suggested: Kat in New Alexandria, Jun in the Nightfall ridges, Jorge at the Sabre launch site, Emile at the shipyard, Carter at the Sword Base hills or the farms. One per district, never in the start district. |
| Survivor rescues + evac points | 3-5 survivor spots and 1 evac pad per district | Survivors are escorted to the evac pad (a Pelican lands). |
| Fallen marine unit stories | 3-5 per district | Last stands with a few bodies and part-full rigs; they never respawn. The ONLY ammo-bearing story set; every other story or container stays empty (no world loot). |
| Purge goals | The Powerhouse dam + substations | Earlier W16 goals; the dam fits Powerhouse. |
| Ops start points (R14 + sortie option) | 1 per campaign op | Near the mission's own start. |

## Conflicts and asks
1. NO DIGGING vs BASE BUILDING: claims, rooms and outposts need block placement (fixtures, walls, sandbags). Base sites and outposts must be buildable zones, or rooms switch to fixed Halo rooms with upgrade slots. This is Josiah's call: suggest asking him with R12.
2. R12 fixed sites vs today's "claim anywhere": fine if he picks (b) and the outposts stay free-placed in purged districts.
3. True scale (R17): the room detector's 3x3 minimum and doorway sizes need retuning for 2.1 m Spartans.
4. R16 old-county fallback: these placements would need old-county equivalents, or the purge layer only runs in the Reach world. Suggest the latter.
5. Purge map shading (P2 B) uses the district outlines, so keep the districts as clean closed shapes.
