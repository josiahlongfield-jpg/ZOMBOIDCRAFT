Rebuilt from your corridor shot: more legs than body.   • A small, tapered body of overlapping jagged plates, rusted and full of holes, with long spines swept back along the ridge and a spike for a tail.
  • Four long steel legs: a short thigh up to a gear knee high above the body, then a long shin with toothed armour and a piston, running down into a barbed point.
  • A blackened skull pushed forward in ringed collars, two red ring eyes stacked on its left, a broad mouth of long teeth.
  • Two small scythe arms folded under the head like a mantis's, and red sinew and black cables looping out to the legs.
