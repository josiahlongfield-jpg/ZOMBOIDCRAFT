What you're looking at

    How different is it?

    • Shape. Tomb's body has about five times the polygons (5,172 triangles for the woman against Zomboid's 984, and 4,590 for the man against 916). The extra detail goes into the face, hands, chest, hips and knees, so the silhouette is rounder and less blocky. Height and proportions match Zomboid's almost exactly, so Zomboid's clothes and animations fit it. Wireframe shows the difference best.
    • Skin. Tomb's textures are 512px against Zomboid's 256px, with painted muscle, collarbones and shading. Its own underwear is a black set, a different style from Zomboid's light blue one.
    • Goth. The same body with pale skin, dark lips and eyes and black underwear.
    • Faces. Tomb adds 9 faces for men and 9 for women. Each comes in Zomboid's 5 skin tones (only tone 1 is shown here). It also has makeup, muscle and stubble overlays that I haven't loaded yet.
    • The two female mods. Detailed Player Texture and Evil's Ink use the same texture layout as Tomb, so they fit Tomb's body as well as Zomboid's. They're shown on Tomb's body here.
    • Women's base set. Every woman, Zomboid's included, now wears Tomb's black bra and briefs as her base set. The men keep their own.
    • Tomb's DOLL and NUDE variants are left out on purpose.
