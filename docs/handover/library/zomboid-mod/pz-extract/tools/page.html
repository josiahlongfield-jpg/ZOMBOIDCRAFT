What you're looking at

  • The body, skin, jeans, leather jacket, red T-shirt, socks, trainers and messy hair are Zomboid's own files. The blood and grime are Zomboid's overlays, applied the way it dirties a zombie's clothes.
  • The animations are Zomboid's real clips, unedited. The ground moves at the speed the feet are planted, so the walk reads the way it would in the world.
  • Height is set so he stands about 1.9 blocks tall, the same as our zombies now. Each ground tile is one block.
  • In the game the outfit would roll from Zomboid's outfit lists (409 outfits in your install), so this is one example out of many.
  • Shooting reactions, falls, crawling and dismemberment aren't in this preview. Zomboid has clips for most of those as well.
