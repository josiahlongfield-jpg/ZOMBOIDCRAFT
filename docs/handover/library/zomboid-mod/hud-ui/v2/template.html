Field kit HUD

Mockup 2 for ZomboidCraft. Bottom left is your own character: its head follows the pointer, and hovering over its face makes it look straight at you. In game it follows where you look. Each piece stays hidden until you use it or something about it changes, then fades back out. Danger keeps a piece up: bleeding, a bad moodle, low health, or aiming. The belt items are the mod's real 3D models. Press 1 to 5 or scroll over the frame to draw, and hold Left Alt to peek.
