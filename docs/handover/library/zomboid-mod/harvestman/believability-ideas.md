# Harvestman: ideas to make it more believable (reply to Josiah, 2026-10-28 23:31)

None of these are built yet.

1. Warning signs before you see it: lights flicker or burst near it (it kills light to stay hidden), dust falls when it crosses a ceiling above you, fresh claw marks and drag trails along the routes it uses, so you can track it.
2. Sound that tells a story: legs clack on metal, creak on wood, go quiet on carpet. The tapping stops when it gets close, so silence becomes the scary part.
3. Weight and body: folds its legs to squeeze through doorways, smashes glass going through windows, knocks loose items off shelves, floors creak, feet leave marks.
4. Careful hunting: stops at doorways and peeks round corners before going in, flinches from a torch beam, pulls back to lick its wounds when hurt.
5. Small movements when it's still: twitches and breathes, body tilts with slopes, legs settle unevenly.
6. Fix stairs: it still gets stuck in house stairwells.

Recommended: 1, 2 and 6.
