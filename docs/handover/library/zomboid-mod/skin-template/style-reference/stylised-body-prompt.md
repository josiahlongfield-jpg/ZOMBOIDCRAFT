# Stylised zombie body prompt (matches the 3D clothing kits)

Attach three images to the art AI, in this order:
1. `zombie-turnaround-template.png` (the layout to fill in, same as before)
2. `style-overview.png` (six outfits, showing the look to match)
3. `style-closeup-soldier.png` (close-up of the colour, outline and shading style)

## Prompt

Paint a zombie body skin on the first image (the turnaround template). Keep the exact canvas size (3072 x 2048), the exact positions of the front, back and side views, and the exact block sizes and proportions. Fill every block edge to edge, with no gaps and nothing outside the blocks. Leave the background pure white.

IMPORTANT: the second and third images are a STYLE reference only. Do not copy their clothing, helmets, vests, belts, boots or gear. The body you paint is bare skin plus grey boxer shorts, nothing else.

Match the art style of the second and third images exactly. Those are the 3D clothes that will be worn over this body, so the body has to look like it belongs with them:
- Stylised and cartoonish, but still believable. Think Borderlands or Project Zomboid, not photorealism.
- Chunky, simple shapes with flat colours, plus 2 or 3 hard-edged shade steps per colour. No soft airbrushed gradients, no photo textures, no fine skin pores or realistic wrinkles.
- A thin dark outline only around the outer edge of each body part. No lines where blocks meet, and no outline drawn around each block.
- Visible, slightly pixel-chunky detail at about the same scale as the clothing in the reference.
- A moody, desaturated palette: greyish, sickly olive, bruised purple-grey and dirty brown skin. Dark, but not black.

Body: a slim, shuffling zombie wearing only grey boxer shorts.
- Big, pronounced, simplified features: deep dark eye sockets with pale, flat whites; a sagging open mouth with a few blocky teeth; a broad nose drawn as 2 or 3 shade blocks; short painted-on hair (no 3D hair).
- Mitten hands with no separate fingers. No thumbs.
- Damage drawn as bold graphic shapes in the same flat style: dark bite wounds with a red rim, a few torn-skin patches, bruises, dried blood running down from the mouth and the wounds. Keep the damage readable, not gory photo detail.
- The side view shows one ear. The back shows the back of the head and hair, with no face.

Don't add clothing other than the boxers, text, a logo, a frame, labels or extra figures.
