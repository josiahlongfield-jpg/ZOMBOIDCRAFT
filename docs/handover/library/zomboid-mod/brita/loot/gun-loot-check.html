Magazines, rounds and sounds

  • Plain magazines for the 18 guns leave loot. Brita guns keep their own magazines.
  • Rounds stay where a Brita gun or the Heavy Sniper fires them (9mm, 5.56, .308, .38, .44, .380, .22, 12 gauge, .50, bolts, and the Brita rounds). .30-30 and 40mm grenades leave, since nothing fires them now.
  • Every gunshot, reload and handling sound of the 18 guns leaves the jar. Kept: the Heavy Sniper’s, the minigun’s, and the shared bullet-hit and casing sounds.
  • Where the Brita sound pack lacks a sound (dry fire, some reload clicks), the gun goes quiet for that action until I fill it from your PC.
  • Copies already in your worlds are taken away (question 4). Gun cases found from now on hold Brita guns, by Arsenal’s case lists.
