How it gets built

  1. The plan becomes data

    Places, roads, rivers, lakes, the Knobs and the military land go into one plan file inside the mod. This page draws from the same data, so what you approve here is what gets generated.

  2. A hard edge

    The world border sits at ±8,192 blocks. A double chain-link Exclusion Zone fence runs just inside it, with watchtowers, and an Army checkpoint wherever a road or railway leaves the county. The bridges over the Ohio are blown.

  3. Terrain follows the plan

    Rivers and lakes are carved where they're drawn. Flat bottomland runs along the Ohio and the Salt, the Knobs rise steep and wooded in the south-west, and the farmland rolls gently in between.

  4. Roads from the plan

    Highways, state roads and the railway follow these lines, with bridges where they cross water. The current road grid only fills in side streets inside towns.

  5. Generated buildings

    Each place runs the existing generators: downtowns, towns, suburbs, ports, farms and bases. Louisville is several downtown districts side by side behind the Blockade wall.

  6. Countryside fill

    Farms, gas stations, diners, churches and trailer lots are spread along the roads and through the farmland. Their spots on this map are a preview, and the generator places the real ones.

Your calls

Is 16 km square the right size?

That's about an hour's walk edge to edge, close to the size of Zomboid's map. Going to 24 km mostly adds more countryside between the towns.

I'd keep 16 km and spend the effort on detail.

Keep Zomboid's town names?

Muldraugh, West Point, Riverside, Rosewood, March Ridge and Louisville make it feel like Knox County straight away. We could invent our own instead.

I'd keep them, since the goal is Zomboid in Minecraft.

Where do you start?

Zomboid lets you pick a starting town. We could do the same, with a choice of Muldraugh, West Point, Riverside, Rosewood or March Ridge, or always start in Muldraugh.

I'd offer a choice of starting town.

Same buildings in every world?

With a fixed internal seed, every world is the identical county, so you and your friends learn the same streets and I can hand-tune spots. Otherwise the layout stays fixed but the houses reshuffle each world.

I'd make every world identical.
