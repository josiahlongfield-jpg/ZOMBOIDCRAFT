The Glass Line

A glass tube on the floor of the South Arm, from Mercy General on the island to a new stop at Eastside Quay on the mainland. The city's 80s showpiece, and now the one way off the island that isn't a jammed bridge.

Ride

What you pass

  1. 1Under the el. The tube leaves the island seawall and dives beneath the el viaduct, its piers going by on both sides.
  2. 2Tug and coal barge sunk at the foot of the island's bank, coal spilled across the bed.
  3. 3Sunk freighter lying beside the tube for its whole length, deck awash, its bridge standing out of the water.
  4. 4Under the highway bridge. Cars and a city bus that went off the jammed bridge lie on the bottom.
  5. 5Eastside Quay. The platform runs along the seawall behind one long glass wall onto the channel.
