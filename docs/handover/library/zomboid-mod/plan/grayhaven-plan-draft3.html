Grayhaven County Master Plan

Draft 3 puts your trains in. Downtown Grayhaven is now an island city at the mouth of the Calder, cut off from the mainland by the river’s North and South Arms and reached by bridges. The six subway stops sit on one loop that runs through the middle of the island: Grayhaven Stadium, Broadcast Tower, the Power Station, the Waterfront, Union Station and Mercy General. The other landmarks are scattered between them. The county railway is one loop of about 40 km that reaches every town. It is elevated only on the island, comes down on the bridges, and runs on the ground across the mainland. Union Station is the one place the railway and subway meet. Every reference location has a home

Port and docks

The Grayhaven Megaport has post-Panamax cranes, container stacks and giant cargo ships at the quay. There's also the North Terminal, a shipyard with a ship in dry dock, the refinery jetty, the Old Harbor wharves, and fishing harbours at Cape Merrow and Gull Harbor.

Downtown and the el

Downtown, the Financial District and Old Harbor share Grayhaven Island. The railway runs on girders above the island’s streets and comes down to the ground once it crosses to the mainland.

Skyscraper interiors

Lobbies, offices, stairwells and rooftops in the Grayhaven towers. Riverside and Ashby have smaller towers.

Hospital and police

Mercy General and the Central Precinct are downtown, and St. Mary's is in Uptown. Riverside and Ashby have their own hospitals, and there are field hospitals in the quarantine zones.

Military base and quarantine

Fort Caldwell has its airfield and tank ranges, and the Navy station is on the cape. The stadium evacuation centre, four quarantine zones and the Grayhaven Blockade round it out.

Suburbs

Westgate, Northside and Southport are inner row-house grids. Pinecrest Estates and Ashby Heights are out along the beltway, and Shady Acres is a trailer park.

Small towns

Kingsbridge, Seabright, Millbrook and Fairview, plus the hamlets of Hollow Creek, Dunmore and Timberline.

Countryside farms

A small valley of farms between Ashby and the mountains, and a few on the plateau by the fort.

Subway

One loop under the island through six stops: the stadium, the tower, the power station, the waterfront, Union Station and Mercy General.

Every named place

Each place keeps its type, size and style from the plan, and the buildings inside are generated. The ones tagged new are place types the generator doesn't have yet, so they'd be built as part of this work.

How it gets built

  1. The plan becomes data

    Places, roads, the coastline, rivers, the mountains, the marshes and the military land go into one plan file inside the mod. This page draws from the same data, so what you approve here is what gets generated.

  2. A real sea

    The sea is proper deep water: a sheltered bay for the port, shallows, sandy beaches in the south, cliffs on the cape, seawalls and quays in the city, and two islands. Wrecks lie offshore, and the Navy blockade holds the sea edge.

  3. A hard edge on land

    The world border sits at ±8,192 blocks. A double chain-link Exclusion Zone fence runs just inside it on the land side, with watchtowers and an Army checkpoint wherever a road or railway leaves.

  4. One continuous city

    Grayhaven's districts sit edge to edge on one street grid, so there's no countryside between them. The towers are downtown, then the mid-rises, then the row houses, with factories and the port along the water.

  5. Terrain follows the plan

    The city is flat along the bay. The mountains rise steep and forested in the west, with the dam, the reservoir and the lab up there. The farm valley rolls between them.

  6. Track and stations

    The world lays the track bed for the county loop and the subway tunnels to the size agreed with the train work: a 6-block bed, 6 blocks of clearance, and platforms level with the car floor. Every station gets a call button.

  7. Generated buildings

    Each place runs its generator: downtowns, row houses, ports, factories, towns and bases. Every building gets detailed interiors and themed loot. That work is next.

Your calls

Are the subway stops where you want them?

The six stops are at least about 600 blocks apart, with Union Station in the middle and the Waterfront on the east shore. Drag any of them, and the tunnel follows.

A lap of the subway is about 5 km, roughly five minutes on board.

Is the railway loop right?

It leaves Union Station north over the North Arm, runs out through Uptown, Pinecrest and Fort Caldwell, round the west through Riverside, Millbrook, Dunmore, Fairview and Ashby, back along the south through Hollow Creek, Kingsbridge, Seabright and the airport, and in over the South Arm. That’s 14 stations plus Union Station.

Drag stations to reroute it, or leave a note for a stop to add or cut.

Is 16 km square the right size?

That's about an hour's walk edge to edge, close to the size of Zomboid's map. At this size, Grayhaven alone is about 7 by 9 km.

I'd keep 16 km and spend the effort on detail.

Are the names right?

All the names are made up: Grayhaven, Riverside, Ashby, Seabright, Fort Caldwell, Ravenrock. Rename anything you like in the notes.

The names cost nothing to change until the plan goes into the generator.

Where do you start?

Zomboid lets you pick a starting town. We could offer Riverside, Ashby, Kingsbridge or Seabright, or always start in the same one.

I'd offer a choice, with Grayhaven itself too dangerous to start in.

Same buildings in every world?

With a fixed internal seed, every world is the identical county, so you and your friends learn the same streets and I can hand-tune spots. Otherwise the layout stays fixed but the buildings reshuffle each world.

I'd make every world identical.
