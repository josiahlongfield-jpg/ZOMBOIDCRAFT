Reach World Layout

A brand new 16 km open world, separate from your county world, which stays exactly as it is. Each campaign level sits inside its own real surroundings: the valleys, mountains, bays and coasts that Reach shows past the edge of each mission, taken from the game files and drawn here at true scale. So most of the land between the missions is real Reach scenery, not invented ground. Every mission keeps its full layout, but its invisible walls come out, so you can walk off any mission path into the open land. Gold outlines mark the original playable areas, coloured dots are the missions' own enemy squads, and diamonds are new settlements and bases. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap a place for details.

North is up · 1 block = 1 m

How it all flows together

The levels come from different places on Reach, so landmarks won't match exactly. These rules hide the joins so it plays as one world.

  1. Real surroundings first. Each mission sits inside its own far scenery, so the first few kilometres around every mission are exactly what Reach shows from inside it. That scenery was built to be seen from far away, so it gets a detail pass where you walk: ground textures at full resolution, rocks, trees, grass and props taken from the missions.
  2. No funnelling. Mission invisible walls, kill zones and soft ceilings are removed, and the surrounding landscape gets solid ground, so every mission can be entered and left from any side.
  3. One ground between. Where two surroundings meet, the land eases from one to the other over about 1.5 km (the faded edges on the map), matching heights so there are no cliffs or steps at a seam.
  4. Same textures across the seam. Inside the blend band the ground uses the level's own grass, rock, snow or sand textures and fades into the region's textures, which are also taken from the nearest Reach level.
  5. One sky, one storm. All Halo skyboxes are dropped. The endless rain sky, fog and lightning cover everything, and each level's lighting is matched to the same overcast light.
  6. One distant horizon. Past the 16 km edge, a single ring of mountains and sea, built from the outer parts of the same Reach scenery, surrounds the world.
  7. One sea level. Water in Aszod, Boardwalk, Tempest, Forge World and Exodus is set to the world's sea level, and coasts are finished with Forge World cliffs and beaches.
  8. Roads join up. Each level's road and path exits connect to the world road network: dirt Warthog tracks in the country and New Alexandria highways near the city.
  9. Hard edges hidden. Where a level ends in a wall or drop, rock outcrops, tree lines and Forerunner pieces from Forge World cover the edge.
  10. Sitting at the right height. Each level is raised or lowered so its ground meets the region's ground. New Alexandria is about 1 km from the water to the highest tower, taller than a normal Minecraft world (384 blocks), so the new world uses a taller custom height (up to 4,064 blocks).

What fills the rest

Real Reach landscape (the missions plus their own surroundings) covers about km² of the 268 km² world. The rest is new land in the style of the nearest region:

    Settlements, mini bases and activities

    These are spread through the open land (diamonds on the map): UNSC firebases to retake and hold, Covenant forward bases and dig sites to clear, farmsteads and relay outposts, an evacuation port, a shipbreaker camp and survivor holdouts. The PLANS list (base sites, pod drops, Phantom drops, Noble rescues) goes on top. Zomboid zombie hordes roam everywhere, led by the Covenant squads.

      Grand designs, a starting list

      Big new structures built to Reach's own look and detail, from its kit pieces and textures. These are starting points for when we brainstorm.

      • Covenant excavation spire. A Spire-style tower driven into a glassed crater, with a gravity lift, shield gate and Engineer-run dig pit below. It's a multi-stage assault.
      • Covenant staging ground. A cliff-top Covenant camp: Phantom landing pads, Shade nests, Wraith pens, supply pods and a holo command tent. It keeps being reinforced until you clear it.
      • Downed corvette. A second Covenant corvette crashed across a valley, built from the corvette kit, so the interior can be explored at ground level.
      • Glassing front. A scorched band of glassed ground and melted towns on the edge of the map, with a cruiser overhead. It's the most dangerous area.
      • Orbital defence generator. A huge UNSC power complex dug into a mountain. Its reactor halls, cooling towers and maglev freight line power the city's defences, and it's a headline purge objective.
      • ONI black site. A hidden base under the mountains, like Sword Base and Halsey's lab: hangar doors in the rock, labs, holding cells and vaults. It's a natural home for the redressed bunkers.
      • Firebase network. Ridge-top firebases with Pelican pads, sandbag lines and turrets, linked by Warthog roads. They're the bases you take back and upgrade.
      • Spaceport and evac terminal. A civilian spaceport outside New Alexandria with launch gantries, terminal halls and abandoned queues. It holds survivor rescues and a last stand.

      Not placed: Noble Actual is the opening cutscene, and its far scenery is part of the horizon. The Pillar of Autumn's cutscene section is not playable.

      Orbit · its own dimension

      The Long Night of Solace corvette

      The ship is a separate dimension, like the Nether, so it costs nothing in the overworld. From the ground you see a lightweight model of the hull holding about 1.5 km above the Sabre launch facility. From the ship you look down on Reach's planet, painted with our world map and refreshed when the world changes. The mission's interiors are drawn below at their real positions inside the hull, so they already join from bow to middle. The mission never builds the back half, so Zealot becomes the aft decks.

      Before the build starts

      • Sword Base and The Package are the same valley in the game, once in summer and once in winter. I've put The Package's snowy copy up in the north-east mountains so both are playable. The other option is to keep one valley and put The Package's unique parts in it: Halsey's lab, the ice caves and the snowbound outpost.
      • Enemy squads are shown for Long Night of Solace, Winter Contingency and Sword Base. The other missions' squads come off your PC next time it's on, and they'll be drawn the same way.
      • Two worlds. At world creation you pick the county world or the Reach world. The county world keeps all its towns, roads, trains, bunkers and landmarks, and nothing in it changes.
      • Spots. Any place you want moved: tell me the name and where it should go, for example "put Exodus next to Aszod".
      • Choices already in the plan doc that change this map: R1 (where multiplayer maps go), R3 (Anniversary remakes, hidden here as recommended), R13 (roads, flights and whether the old train becomes a Reach maglev), R15 (old landmarks rebuilt inside New Alexandria). R16 and R19 no longer apply now that the county world stays as its own world.
