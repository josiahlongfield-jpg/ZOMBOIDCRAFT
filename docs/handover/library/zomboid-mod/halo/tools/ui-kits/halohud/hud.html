Spartan · Reach style

  • Shield bar top centre, with health pips under it. A hit flashes it red, and it recharges after a pause.
  • Weapon top right: the mag as a big number and a row of rounds, then what you carry, then your second weapon and grenades.
  • Motion tracker bottom left, 25 m. Red is enemies, yellow is squad mates. Anyone walking or sprinting shows; crouch-walking hides you.
  • Armour ability beside the tracker, with its recharge.
  • Hunt target top left, with how much of the district the Covenant still hold.

Operator · Tarkov style

  • Almost nothing on screen. Stamina bars bottom centre only while they're not full.
  • Status icons bottom left only while something's wrong (bleeding, fracture, pain, infected).
  • Health body only while you hold Tab, or for a moment after a hit.
  • Gun line bottom right after you reload, switch weapon or change fire mode: name, fire mode and mag / carried, then it fades.

What leaves the screen

The moodle bar, belt hotbar, wristwatch, skill tape, level-up card and the field kit body figure all go, since skills, needs and world loot are retired. Rain on your face stays for both. The full list of screens and the picks for you is in the write-up.
