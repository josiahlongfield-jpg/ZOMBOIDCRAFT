Tarkov gear ledger

Every Tarkov wearable going into ZomboidCraft as loot, with Tarkov's own pockets, armour and rarity carried over. Boss, event, Halloween and streamer pieces are already out. Clothing is loot too; the free Standard, Day off, Winter, Instructor, Special Ops and Nord outfits are the starting choices.

Rigs

Each rig keeps Tarkov's exact pocket layout. A 1×2 pocket takes a rifle mag or a grenade; a 2×2 takes meds, a flask or a radio. Rig pockets are reachable while moving and without opening your pack, so reloading from a rig is quicker than reloading from a bag.

Armoured rigs

These count as armour and a rig at once, so they take both slots and block a separate vest, as in Tarkov.

Backpacks

Tarkov grid sizes. Bigger packs weigh more and slow your turn speed. Opening one means stopping, as in Zomboid, and a full pack adds to Zomboid's heavy-load moodle.

Armour and plates

Each piece covers Tarkov's zones (chest, sides, collar, groin...), mapped to our body parts. Bullets use its armour class and durability. Against zombies, any covered part blocks bites and scratches until the armour wears down. Plates slide in and out at the kit.

Helmets

Zones (crown, nape, ears, eyes, jaw), Tarkov class, ricochet chance. Some block headsets or face covers. Mounts take visors, mandibles and night vision.

Headsets, face, eyes

Headsets make footsteps and groans louder and cut gunfire to a safe level. Gas masks and respirators filter spore haze and smoke. Balaclavas keep you warm. Eyewear stops blood splash, and ballistic eyewear stops fragments.

Armbands

A team colour on your arm, so squadmates can tell you apart in co-op with friendly fire on.

Clothing

Tops and trousers from Tarkov's USEC, BEAR and Scav wardrobes. Each keeps its own look and adds Zomboid warmth. The rarer the outfit, the later Tarkov unlocks it.

Rarity

Tarkov's own rarity and price set the tier: Common, Scarce, Rare, Prized, Grail. Class 5 armour is at least Prized, and class 6 is Grail.
