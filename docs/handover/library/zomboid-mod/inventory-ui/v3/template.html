Minecraft underneath

  • Same slot grid, click to pick up and place, shift-click, right-click to drop one, hotbar keys, durability bars and the character that turns to watch your cursor.
  • Creative-style tabs along the top switch between bags and nearby containers.

Zomboid on top

  • Every bag has slots and a weight limit, and cuts what its contents weigh on you. Over 8 kg brings Heavy Load.
  • School pack 7 kg, hiking pack 18, rucksack 28, duffel 24 (carried in the other hand), plate carrier 5, fanny pack 2.

Mood

  • The world stays visible behind the kit, blurred and dark, with rain on the lens. A torch glow follows your cursor.
  • Panels change material with what you open: canvas for bags, scratched steel for shelves, wood for counters, wet concrete for the floor.

Weapon bench

Only the bare gun body is solid. Each empty socket is a green see-through part with a crosshair, and turns solid once a part is fitted. Magazine and stock come off too.
