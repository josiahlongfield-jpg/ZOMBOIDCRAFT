Parts on hand

How it plays

  • Capacity is weight, not slot count. Each bag holds a set weight and cuts what its contents weigh on you.
  • Over 8 kg you get Heavy Load: slower, more stamina per swing. Past 12 it gets worse.
  • Bags are real items. Drop one and it lands on the floor with everything inside.
  • Sizes: school backpack 7 kg, hiking pack 18, military rucksack 28 (carries best), fanny pack 2.
  • A duffel holds 24 kg but rides in your other hand, so you can't hold a rifle with both hands until you put it down.

Quick slots

  • Hands, Back and Belt hold weapons. The hotbar keys swap between them, with a short draw time like Zomboid.
  • Belt takes one-handed weapons only. Back takes long guns and two-handers.

Weapon bench

  • Sockets: optic, muzzle, under-barrel, magazine and stock. Only parts that fit light up.
  • Only the bare body is solid. Every empty socket shows as a green see-through part with a crosshair, like your reference, and turns solid once something is fitted.
  • An optic removes the iron sights. The parts here are rough shapes; the real models come from the weapons work.
