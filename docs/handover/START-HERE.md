## The project in one paragraph
ZomboidCraft turns Minecraft into Project Zomboid: a hand-built county (Grayhaven city, towns, airport, army airfield,
dam, prison, shipyard, mall, hospital, university, stadium, rail/subway incl. the Glass Line underwater tube...) at
Project Zomboid B42 detail with custom furniture and no vanilla blocks or items, Zomboid zombies (PZ bodies, behaviour,
population, hordes that never vanish), survival needs, loot with rarities, cars, trains with OTTO, aircraft, the
Harvestman, companions, base building, and night-heavy "endless night" weather. Late on, the weapons moved from Brita's
pack to Tarkov guns, and the "purge" re-steer moved Halo/Tarkov work into Endless Night.
