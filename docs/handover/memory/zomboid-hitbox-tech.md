Built 2026-10-27 in the characters thread after Josiah asked (06:39) to "make sure they cover the character well". Uses [[zomboid-dismember-tech]] and [[zomboid-zbody-tech]].

- `combat/BodyCaps.java` is GENERATED by `tools/zombies/hitcaps/run.sh` (about 15 min, 4 cores, -Xmx10g). Rerun whenever zbody/*.bin or ZMove poses change. 19 tables (IDLE, SHAMBLE, ALERT, CHASE, ATTACK x4 at 0.5 s, DOWN, crawl rest/slow/fast/alert, CRAWL_GRAB x2, HOP, HOP_REACH, STAND1, STAND1_REACH), 13 capsules each, 8 floats (part, a xyz, b xyz, r) in the zombie frame (x left, y up, z forward). Head = part 0 twice. One-leg tables have the right leg gone; Dismember mirrors them for the left.
- Fitter: ray-casts the textured mesh from 24 views (0.02-block pixels) over several animation phases, fits on civilian/soldier/firefighter/survivor, tests on police/construction/jogger/doctor. Score rewards right part (head x2), penalises empty-space hits (head ones most). Each table's comment line gives caught / right part / extra vs the old tables.
- Before: only 54-77% of shots touching the drawn body registered. After: about 82-95% caught.
- `combat/BodyTrack.java`: per-walker 32-tick ring (x, y, z, yBodyRot, pose), pose picked like ZBodyAnimator (walk speed eased min(dist*4,1) at 0.4; moving > 0.08, > 0.02 on one leg; attack 2 s from the swing's rising edge). Dismember.shot rewinds round(ping/50) + limbs.lagTicks (4) ticks, tries lag, lag+1, lag-1; during a 4-tick pose blend it tests both tables.
- Offline overlay sheet (scratchpad only, not in repo): jtest/src/Sheet.java draws the textured zombie with capsule stadiums, front/side, six poses.
